THE TEEN
MONEY GUIDE

01

WHERE CAN TEENS WORK?

Getting a job is a great way to earn your own money and gain experience. Here are some jobs teens can look for:

Retail

Clothing stores, grocery stores, and shops.

Restaurants

Fast food, cafés, and restaurants.

Recreation

Pools, camps, sports centres, and programs.

Babysitting

Look after children for families in your community.

Pet Sitting

Walk dogs or care for pets while owners are away.

Tutoring

Help younger students with school subjects.

TIP: Look for jobs with flexible hours so you can balance school, work, and your personal life.
02

HOW MUCH SHOULD I SAVE?

$300+

Start building your savings.

GRADE 11

$750+

03

HOW TO MAKE A BUDGET

A budget helps you decide where your money should go instead of wondering where it went.

1

Know Your Income

Figure out how much money you receive each month.

2

List Your Expenses

Write down everything you spend money on.

3

Separate Needs & Wants

Needs are important. Wants are things you can live without.

4

Set a Savings Goal

Choose an amount you want to save each month.

5

Track Your Spending

Check your budget regularly to stay on track.

INCOME − EXPENSES = MONEY LEFT
04

SMART MONEY HABITS

Small habits can make a big difference over time.

EARN

Look for ways to earn money through jobs or side work.

SAVE

Put some of your money aside instead of spending it all.

SPEND SMART

Compare prices and think before making purchases.

SET GOALS

Have something specific you are saving your money for.

“The goal isn't to never spend money. It's to learn how to manage it.”
05

YOUR MONEY. YOUR FUTURE.

Learning how to manage money now can help you become more confident with your finances in the future.
